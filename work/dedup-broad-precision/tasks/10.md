# Task 10: Post-deploy precision verification

## Required Skills

Before starting, load:
- `/skill:post-deploy-qa` — [SKILL.md](../../../.claude/skills/post-deploy-qa/SKILL.md)

## Description

Verify the feature against naturally occurring production traffic during the approved observation window. This is the independent check against corpus overfitting and must not inject test articles, trigger publication, or expose operator data.

## What to do

- Observe naturally occurring E014 decisions and classify each as correct duplicate, false flag, or unresolved using public article evidence.
- Compare the daily article-level suppression count with the bounded per-candidate diagnostics and investigate mismatches.
- Watch E015 and E016 for an unexpected increase or changed behavior and trigger the documented rollback/toggle mitigation if safety thresholds are breached.
- Record the evidence window, sanitized aggregate counts, anomalies and final milestone verdict in the post-deploy QA report.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] No test article, Telegram test message, forced scheduler tick or publication is injected.
- [ ] Every naturally occurring E014 in the observation window is classified or explicitly marked unresolved with a reason.
- [ ] Daily suppression totals reconcile with diagnostic article units without copying private/raw logs.
- [ ] No unexpected E015/E016 behavior is observed; any safety regression triggers mitigation and blocks milestone completion.
- [ ] The final report contains sanitized aggregate evidence and a clear accepted, extended-observation, or rollback verdict.

## Details

**Files:** Reads sanitized deployment evidence and live operator-visible outcomes; writes only `work/dedup-broad-precision/logs/working/post-deploy-qa-report.json`.

**Dependencies:** Task 9 must deploy successfully. Live classification needs ordinary production intake and therefore may span multiple scheduled ticks.

**Edge cases:** No E014 occurs during the window, an article cannot be confidently classified, suppression count is non-zero with no retained diagnostic evidence, E016 is unrelated to the feature, or rollback occurs mid-window.

**Implementation hints:** Separate event time from report time; aggregate rather than copy raw logs; compare against the user-spec thresholds and treat inconclusive evidence honestly.
